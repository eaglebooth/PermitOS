# PermitOS logo-derived design guidelines

Source: user-provided PermitOS logo, visually inspected at 1254 x 1254.

## Brand cues

- Mood: calm environmental authority, documentary rigor, protective control.
- Motifs: permit sheet, checklist, water drop, leaf and shield.
- Avoid a dark cyber-dashboard or copying earlier GenLayer products.
- Direction: editorial permit docket with paper surfaces, registry stamps and a
  vertical intake timeline.

## Palette

- `#063f3a`: deep permit green; primary text and navigation.
- `#0f7658`: authority green; positive accents.
- `#1685bd`: water blue; active controls and links.
- `#dff3df`: pale mint; soft evidence panels.
- `#f7f4ea`: warm paper; page background.
- `#fffdf7`: document surface.
- `#d8d8c9`: quiet rule/border.
- `#d06b3f`: restrained warning/action accent.

## Typography and geometry

- Display: Fraunces-style editorial serif for major headings.
- UI/body: Manrope-style humanist sans; monospace only for IDs/digests.
- Spacing scale: 6, 10, 16, 24, 36, 56, 84 px.
- Corners: 4 px for documentary fields, 18–28 px for major surfaces.
- Borders: thin green-gray rules; subtle offset shadows, no glassmorphism.
- Motion: one restrained page-entry sequence and clear hover/focus movement.

## Responsive composition

- Landing page: asymmetric editorial hero with a large cropped logo tile.
- Console: centered 1280 px docket, left lifecycle rail, main action surface,
  right authoritative readback on wide screens; single column under 980 px.
- Never permit horizontal overflow; long hashes and URLs wrap anywhere.
